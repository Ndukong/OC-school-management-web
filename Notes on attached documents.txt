4. Mark sheets: Here are some guides:
- [TERM] = FIRST TERM, SECOND TERM, THIRD TERM, ANNUAL
- Subject codes are gotten from the subjects and competencies template (e.g. ENL, FRE, MAT, etc.). Subj. coef are coefficients for that class.
- Subj. 1 to subj. n are all the subjects offered in that class. Add as many columns in the table. The mark entered here should be the average for the term (or annual) multiplied by the subject coefficient (weighted mark). Fail marks (average<10) should be in red.
- Coef is the sum of coefficients of subjects that student actually has a mark in (including zero as a mark). If there is no mark for that subject, its coefficient is ignored.
- Score is the sum of all weighted scores that student has.
- Av. is the average of the student for that term (or annual). If <10, red. 
- Rank is student rank for the term. Failed => red
- Remark: use Passed (P) or Failed (F) for terms and Promoted/Repeat for annual (use promotion mark in case of annual). Use red for failed or repeat.
