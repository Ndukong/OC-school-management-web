Quick Setup Checklist
  1. School profile — name, matricule, contact, bilingual letterhead, logo & seal.
  2. Academic terms — create the year’s terms and mark one current.
  3. Classes & streams — add Form 1–6 and streams (A, B, Commercial…).
  4. Subjects — add the subject catalogue, then assign coefficients per class.
  5. Competencies — record subject competencies per term & form level.
  6. Teachers — add staff, then link users & assign subjects.
  7. Users — create accounts for staff and link them to teacher records.
  8. PTA configuration — rubric heads, sub-heads, per-class dues and fee types.
  9. Conduct thresholds — set discipline levels for the class council.
  10. Done → import students, enter marks, compute results and generate reports.
